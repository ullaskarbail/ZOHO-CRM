# Zoho CRM Reports & Dashboards Specification

This document details the configuration of standard and custom reports, analytics components, KPI metrics, and dashboards built within Zoho CRM for School Management.

---

## 1. Dashboard: School Executive Operational Summary

### KPI Cards (Top Banner)
- **Total Enrolled Students**: Sum of active records in `Students` module.
- **YTD Admission Conversion Rate**: `(Converted Enquiries / Total Webform Enquiries) * 100`.
- **Average School Attendance Rate (%)**: Average of `Attendance_Percentage` across all active students.
- **Total Fee Revenue Collected ($)**: Sum of `Fee_Paid_Amount` across all active students.
- **Total Outstanding Fees ($)**: Sum of `Outstanding_Fee_Balance` across all active students.
- **At-Risk Students Count**: Count of active students with `Early_Warning_Level == 'High / Critical'`.

---

## 2. Admission Management Reports & Analytics

### Report A: Admission Enquiry Conversion Funnel
- **Report Type**: Matrix / Funnel Chart on `Leads` module.
- **Grouping**: Grouped by `Lead_Status` (`New Enquiry` -> `Contacted` -> `Campus Tour` -> `Entrance Test` -> `Offer Sent` -> `Admission Confirmed`).
- **Metrics**: Count of leads at each stage, drop-off rate, and average days to convert.
- **Purpose**: Helps the admissions team identify bottlenecks in parent conversion.

### Report B: Lead Source Performance Matrix
- **Report Type**: Summary Chart on `Leads` module.
- **Grouping**: Grouped by `Lead_Source` (`School Webform`, `Walk-in`, `Referral`, `Online Ad`).
- **Metrics**: Total enquiries generated vs. confirmed admissions percentage per source.
- **Purpose**: Evaluates marketing ROI for school recruitment campaigns.

---

## 3. Student Attendance Analytics

### Report C: Low Attendance Defaulters Alert List (< 75%)
- **Report Type**: Tabular Report on `Students` module.
- **Filters**: `Attendance_Percentage < 75.0` AND `Student_Status == 'Active'`.
- **Columns Displayed**: `Student_ID`, `Full_Name`, `Current_Class`, `Current_Section`, `Attendance_Percentage`, `Parent_Name`, `Parent_Phone`, `Parent_Email`.
- **Automation Action**: Auto-scheduled weekly export emailed to Class Teachers & School Principal.

### Report D: Daily Class-wise Attendance Heatmap
- **Report Type**: Matrix Report on `Daily_Attendance` module.
- **Rows**: `Class` & `Section`.
- **Columns**: `Attendance_Date`.
- **Summary**: Total Present Count vs. Total Absent Count per day.

---

## 4. Examination & Academic Performance Reports

### Report E: Class Subject Grade Distribution & Average GPA
- **Report Type**: Summary Matrix on `Student_Exam_Results` module.
- **Grouping**: Grouped by `Class`, `Exam`, and `Subject`.
- **Metrics**:
  - Average `Percentage_Score` per subject.
  - Count of grades (`A+`, `A`, `B`, `C`, `D`, `F`).
  - Overall Pass Percentage per subject.
- **Purpose**: Identifies subjects with low student performance requiring curriculum review.

### Report F: Top Academic Performers & Honor Roll
- **Report Type**: Ranked List on `Students` module.
- **Filters**: `Current_GPA >= 3.5` ORDER BY `Current_GPA` DESC.
- **Columns**: `Student_ID`, `Full_Name`, `Current_Class`, `Current_GPA`, `Attendance_Percentage`.

---

## 5. Fee Collection & Financial Dashboards

### Report G: Fee Collection Aging & Defaulter Breakdown
- **Report Type**: Matrix Report on `Students` module.
- **Grouping**: Grouped by `Current_Class` and `Fee_Status` (`Paid`, `Partially Paid`, `Overdue`).
- **Metrics**: Sum of `Total_Fee_Amount`, `Fee_Paid_Amount`, and `Outstanding_Fee_Balance`.
- **Visual Chart**: Stacked Bar Chart showing Paid vs. Outstanding fees per grade level.

### Report H: Monthly Fee Payment Transactions Register
- **Report Type**: Summary Report on `Fee_Payments` module.
- **Grouping**: Grouped by `Payment_Date` (Month) and `Payment_Mode`.
- **Metrics**: Count of payments, Total collected amount, Payment mode breakdown (Online Creator Portal vs. Bank Transfer vs. Cash).

---

## 6. Early Warning System (EWS) Risk Dashboard

### Report I: EWS At-Risk Student Matrix
- **Report Type**: Dashboard Donut Chart & Detail Grid.
- **Grouping**: Grouped by `Early_Warning_Level` (`Low`, `Moderate`, `High / Critical`).
- **Metrics**: Count of students per risk tier.
- **Intervention Tracker**: List of open counselor tasks generated by the EWS engine.
